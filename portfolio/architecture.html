Assemblies, sub-assemblies, and stampings. Process sheets, negotiated steel, BOMs, and engineering changes land in one system. A numbered run closes the plant and the month.

How a month closes

01

Workbooks

Process sheets, BOMs, steel terms, and engineering changes arrive as Excel.

02

Readiness

Plant, year, and month. Missing prices or fees stop the run.

03

Steel

Base price plus thickness, width, and zinc policies. The month’s steel.

04

Stamped part

Material, conversion, die, and offal on each stamping.

05

Assembly

Weld and purchased parts roll up. Finance and procurement read one price.

06

Explanation

Plain language over the closed result. Every figure already exists on the grid.

One numbered, timed run. The engine orders the work. Each calculator owns its formula. Original workbooks stay archived.
